# CareLabs — Comprehensive Lab Testing — Patientory

> CareLabs provides access to a wide range of diagnostic tests with detailed biomarker analysis for informed healthcare decisions — with results synced straight to your Patientory Wallet. Choose a test, provide a sample at a local collection center or with an at-home kit, and receive detailed results within 24 hours.

Page: https://patientory.com/carelabs

## How CareLabs works

1. **Choose your test** — select from a comprehensive test catalog.
2. **Provide a sample** — visit a local collection center or use an at-home kit.
3. **Get results** — detailed results within 24 hours.
4. **Understand results** — detailed insights and recommendations, securely available in the Patientory app.

## What it's for

Home-based diagnostic testing that helps identify potential health concerns early, track wellness progress, and support preventive healthcare strategies — a deeper understanding of your overall well-being through detailed biomarker analysis.
