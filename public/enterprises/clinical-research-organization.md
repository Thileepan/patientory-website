# Patientory for Pharmaceuticals & Clinical Research Organizations (CROs)

> Accelerate diversity in clinical trials and optimize R&D: advanced tools and data-driven insights to accelerate trials, optimize processes, and achieve better outcomes — while reducing costs and ensuring compliance.

## Key benefits for CROs

- **Accelerated trial timelines** — faster patient recruitment and streamlined data collection
- **Enhanced data accuracy** — real-time data capture; FDA, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance
- **Patient engagement** — wearable integration and reduced dropout rates
- **Trial management** — automated workflows, digital patient records, integrated communications
- **Data analytics** — real-time insights, progress monitoring, outcome tracking

## How Patientory empowers CROs

- **Patient recruitment & onboarding** — quick identification and enrollment of eligible participants from the Patientory user base (see CareTrials: https://patientory.com/caretrials.md)
- **Wearable integration** — real-time health metrics monitoring
- **Virtual trial capabilities** — support for hybrid and decentralized trial models
- **Efficient data collection** — automated capture with real-time analysis
- **Regulatory compliance** — HIPAA-compliant data security

## Why CROs choose Patientory

Faster, more efficient trials (streamlined processes, reduced recruitment time), reduced costs (seamless integration, less admin work), data-driven decisions (real-time analytics, study optimization, enhanced safety), and future-proof research (decentralized trials, scalable, digital-first) — reducing time-to-market for new therapies with improved data integrity.
