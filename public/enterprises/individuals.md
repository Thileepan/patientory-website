# Patientory for Individuals — Secure Your Health Data

> Take control of your healthcare journey with the Patientory Wallet: secure, decentralized health data management. Manage medical records, track health, and connect with providers — all in one safe platform, with $PTOY token rewards for healthy behavior.

Page: https://patientory.com/enterprises/individuals

## Why choose the Patientory Wallet

- **Data security you can trust** — health data encrypted and stored on the blockchain, with you in full control of access
- **Global accessibility** — access your records anytime, anywhere: new doctors, international travel, emergencies
- **Personalized health insights** — track wellness goals with real-time insights from connected health devices
- **Chronic disease management** — prevent and control chronic conditions with AI Health Coach specialty programs
- **Reward incentives** — earn $PTOY tokens by completing daily health goals and making your data actionable
- **Smart health tracking** — sync wearables and health apps for comprehensive real-time monitoring

## How it works

1. **Download the Patientory Wallet** — available on iOS and Android (https://patientory.com/download)
2. **Create your secure profile** — your data is encrypted using blockchain technology
3. **Upload your health records** — medical history, lab results, prescriptions, and more
4. **Control and share** — decide who can view your records and manage permissions with a tap

## Who benefits

People managing chronic conditions (quick access to medical history), global travelers (health information anywhere in the world), fitness enthusiasts (comprehensive wellness tracking), and privacy-conscious users (advanced blockchain security).
