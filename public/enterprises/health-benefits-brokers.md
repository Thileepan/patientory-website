# Patientory for Health & Benefits Brokers

> Offer your clients a cutting-edge, data-driven healthcare solution that improves health outcomes, reduces costs, and empowers individuals to take control of their well-being — while enhancing your value proposition as a broker.

Page: https://patientory.com/enterprises/health-benefits-brokers

## Key benefits for brokers

- **Cost savings for clients** — preventative health services and chronic disease management that prevent costly medical interventions
- **Comprehensive programs** — from weight management to sexual health
- **Seamless integration** — with existing health plans and insurance
- **Data-driven insights** — real-time health tracking and analytics
- **Personalized care & coaching** — customized wellness plans and nutrition coaching

## Programs you can offer clients

- **Chronic Disease Prevention & Management** — tailored lifestyle plans, wearable integration, medication support, nutrition coaching
- **Weight Management & Wellness** — wearable tracking, personalized coaching, GLP-1 medications, at-home lab tests
- **Sexual & Reproductive Health** — at-home testing kits, hormone balance tracking, personalized wellness plans
- **Preventive Health & Screening** — health screenings, laboratory tests, risk assessments, early detection
- **Employee Wellness Programs** — cost reduction strategies, health education, wellness activities, progress tracking

## How Patientory works

- **Customizable health kits** — wearables, smart scales, at-home testing kits, and health data tracking tools per program
- **Real-time health monitoring** — activity, weight, blood pressure, and sleep in the Patientory app
- **Personalized coaching** — certified health coaches and nutritionists
- **Benefits integration** — works with employers' existing benefits and insurance plans
- **Comprehensive data analytics** — track outcomes and measure program effectiveness
