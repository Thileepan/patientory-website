## Key benefits for self-insured employers

- **Cost reduction** — proactively manage and reduce healthcare costs through preventative health programs
- **Data-driven insights** — real-time analytics to measure program effectiveness and track spending
- **Comprehensive wellness** — from chronic disease prevention to mental health support
- **Engaged employees** — personalized coaching and health tracking tools
- **Seamless integration** — works with your existing health benefits system

## Health & wellness solutions

- **Chronic Disease Prevention & Management** — conditions like diabetes and heart disease managed with personalized health plans and technology
- **Weight Management & Wellness** — smart technology and expert coaching support
- **Preventive Health & Screening** — early detection through at-home testing and regular screenings
- **Mental Health & Stress Management** — coaching and mindfulness programs
- **Employee Engagement Programs** — personalized tracking, goal-setting, and continuous expert support

## How it works for employers

- **Customizable health kits** — wearables, lab tests, and tracking tools tailored to your workforce
- **Real-time health monitoring** — activity, sleep, weight, and blood pressure through the Patientory platform
- **Personalized coaching** — certified health coaches, nutritionists, and wellness experts
- **Plan integration** — easy integration with existing self-insured health plans
- **Data analytics** — detailed reports to measure program effectiveness
