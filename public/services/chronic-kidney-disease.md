# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management Program — Patientory

> Take charge of your kidney health with Patientory's comprehensive Chronic Kidney Disease Management Program ("Kidney Protect"): an integrated approach combining advanced technology, at-home testing, and personalized coaching to monitor your health, prevent progression, and improve overall well-being. Part of Patientory's Chronic Care Management services.

Page: https://patientory.com/services/chronic-kidney-disease

Chronic Kidney Disease often progresses silently, but early detection and management can make all the difference. This program provides the tools and support to monitor kidney health, control risk factors, and live a healthier life.

## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — sign up and receive a comprehensive toolkit.
2. **Receive your health kit** — Google Fitbit, Blood Pressure Cuff, laboratory tests, and GLP-1 medication delivered to your door.
3. **Track & analyze your health** — sync devices with the Patientory app for health metrics and real-time insights.
4. **Personalized nutrition coaching** — certified nutritionists help optimize your diet and achieve sustainable lifestyle changes.
5. **Ongoing support** — personalized help managing GLP-1 medication, exercise routines, and stress.

## What's included

- **Google Fitbit** — daily activity, heart rate, and sleep tracking
- **Blood Pressure Cuff** — at-home blood pressure monitoring (blood pressure control is central to protecting kidney function)
- **Comprehensive lab tests** — at-home blood tests covering cholesterol, kidney function, and other vital indicators
- **GLP-1 medication** — expert guidance to support weight management and blood pressure control
- **Nutrition coaching** — one-on-one sessions for kidney-friendly meal plans, grocery lists, and lifestyle strategies

## Program benefits

- **Real-time health tracking** — heart rate, blood pressure, activity, and sleep in one view
- **Data-driven insights** — personalized recommendations from wearable data and lab results
- **Expert coaching & support** — guidance, motivation, and accountability from certified coaches
- **GLP-1 medication support** — expert-led medication management
- **Holistic lifestyle changes** — sustainable habits that protect kidney function and overall well-being
