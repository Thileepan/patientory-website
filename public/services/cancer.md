# Proactive Cancer Screening Program — Patientory

> Detect cancer early and take control of your well-being with Patientory's all-inclusive Cancer Screening Program: advanced early-detection screening, convenient at-home testing (including a colon cancer FIT test), and personalized health coaching. Part of Patientory's Chronic Care Management services.

Page: https://patientory.com/services/cancer

Cancer can be a silent disease, but early detection can make all the difference — cancer detected early can often be treated more effectively, leading to better outcomes and higher survival rates.

## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — sign up and receive a comprehensive cancer screening toolkit.
2. **Receive your health kit** — Google Fitbit, Blood Pressure Cuff, comprehensive laboratory tests, and a Colon Cancer FIT Test delivered to your home.
3. **Real-time health tracking** — sync devices with the Patientory app for key health metrics and actionable insights.
4. **Personalized nutrition coaching** — certified nutritionists help optimize your diet for cancer prevention and overall health.
5. **GLP-1 medication support** — guidance on how GLP-1 medications can support your health, particularly weight management.

## What's included

- **Google Fitbit** — daily activity, heart rate, and sleep tracking
- **Blood Pressure Cuff** — regular monitoring for early signs of cardiovascular stress
- **Comprehensive laboratory tests** — at-home sample collection for blood sugar, cholesterol, and other cancer-related health markers
- **Colon Cancer Screening (FIT Test)** — a simple, non-invasive Fecal Immunochemical Test that screens for hidden blood in the stool, an early indicator of colorectal cancer
- **Human Hemoglobin Test** — assess hemoglobin levels to detect anemia and other potential concerns
- **GLP-1 medication guidance** and **personalized nutrition coaching** for a cancer-prevention diet plan

## FAQ

**What is the Colon Cancer FIT Test?** The Fecal Immunochemical Test detects hidden blood in the stool, which can be an early indicator of colorectal cancer. It's easy to perform at home and non-invasive.

**How do the at-home lab tests work?** Collect samples with the provided kits, mail them back in pre-paid envelopes, and access results securely in the Patientory app.

**What are the benefits of GLP-1 medications?** They support weight management — which helps reduce the risk of various cancers — improve metabolic health, and support overall wellness.

**Is my health data secure?** Yes — top-tier encryption protects your health information.
