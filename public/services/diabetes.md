## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — sign up to receive your personalized diabetes management kit and access to expert resources.
2. **Receive your health toolkit** — Google Fitbit, Smart Scale, Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM), at-home diabetes laboratory tests, and GLP-1 medication.
3. **Track & analyze your health** — sync devices with the Patientory app for real-time tracking, insights, and recommendations.
4. **Personalized nutrition coaching** — certified nutritionists build a tailored diet plan to manage blood sugar effectively.
5. **Ongoing support** — health coaches help manage your GLP-1 medication and lifestyle changes.

## FAQ

**What is GLP-1 medication and how does it help?** GLP-1 medications help regulate blood sugar, promote weight loss, and reduce the risk of diabetes complications by enhancing insulin production and lowering appetite.

**Can I use my existing Fitbit or CGM?** Yes — the program integrates with most existing devices.

**How do the at-home lab tests work?** Collect your sample with the provided kit, send it back in the pre-paid envelope, and access results securely in the Patientory app.

**Is my health data secure?** Yes — top-tier encryption keeps personal health information private and secure.
