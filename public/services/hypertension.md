High blood pressure is a silent threat. This program provides the tools and support to lower blood pressure, improve heart health, and build lifelong healthy habits.

## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — join the program and receive everything needed to manage hypertension effectively.
2. **Receive your toolkit** — Google Fitbit, Blood Pressure Cuff, laboratory tests, and GLP-1 medication delivered to your doorstep.
3. **Track your health** — sync devices with the Patientory app for monitoring, real-time data, and actionable insights.
4. **Nutrition coaching** — one-on-one coaching from certified nutritionists for a heart-healthy meal plan.
5. **Ongoing guidance** — expert advice on GLP-1 medication, exercise routines, and stress management.

## FAQ

**How does GLP-1 medication help with hypertension?** GLP-1 medications can assist with weight loss, improve blood pressure, and support cardiovascular health by enhancing insulin sensitivity and reducing appetite.

**Can I use my existing Fitbit or blood pressure monitor?** Yes — the program integrates with most existing devices.

**How do the at-home lab tests work?** Collect your sample with the provided kit, send it back in the pre-paid envelope, and access results securely in the Patientory app.

**Is my health data secure?** Yes — industry-leading encryption protects your health information.
