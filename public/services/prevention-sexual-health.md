Your sexual health is a critical component of your overall wellness. This program provides the tools and resources to monitor your health, detect issues early, and make informed lifestyle changes — all from the comfort of your home.

## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — sign up and receive a comprehensive toolkit.
2. **Receive your health kit** — includes a Google Fitbit, Blood Pressure Cuff, and at-home laboratory tests delivered to your door.
3. **Track & analyze your health** — sync devices with the Patientory app for health metrics and real-time insights.
4. **Personalized nutrition coaching** — certified nutritionists help optimize your diet and achieve sustainable lifestyle changes.
5. **Ongoing support** — personalized guidance on lifestyle, exercise routines, and stress management.
