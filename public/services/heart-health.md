# Heart Health Program — Patientory

> Optimize your cardiovascular health with Patientory's all-inclusive Heart Health Program: remote monitoring from home, personalized medical guidance, GLP-1 medication management, and nutrition coaching. Part of Patientory's Chronic Care Management services.

Page: https://patientory.com/services/heart-health

Heart disease is the leading cause of preventable health issues — but it doesn't have to be. This program offers the tools, resources, and expert support to make sustainable changes that protect your heart.

## How it works

1. **Enroll & get started** — sign up and receive a comprehensive toolkit.
2. **Receive your heart health kit** — Google Fitbit, Blood Pressure Cuff, laboratory tests, and GLP-1 medication delivered to your door.
3. **Track & analyze your health** — sync devices with the Patientory app for health metrics and real-time insights.
4. **Personalized nutrition coaching** — certified nutritionists optimize your diet for heart health.
5. **Ongoing support** — personalized help managing GLP-1 medication, exercise routines, and stress.

## What's included

- **Google Fitbit** — daily activity, heart rate, and sleep quality tracking
- **Blood Pressure Cuff** — at-home blood pressure monitoring
- **Comprehensive lab tests** — at-home blood tests for cholesterol levels, lipid profiles, and other vital heart-health indicators
- **GLP-1 medication** — expert guidance for weight management, blood pressure control, and improved heart function
- **Nutrition coaching** — one-on-one sessions for heart-healthy meal plans, grocery lists, and lifestyle strategies

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to improve blood pressure control and heart health, manage cholesterol and lipid levels, optimize diet and exercise with expert guidance, or use GLP-1 medication for weight and heart-health outcomes — with convenient at-home testing.

## FAQ

**How does GLP-1 medication benefit heart health?** GLP-1 medications can help reduce blood pressure, lower cholesterol, and support weight loss by enhancing insulin sensitivity and curbing appetite.

**Can I use my existing Fitbit or blood pressure monitor?** Yes — the program integrates with most existing devices.

**How do the at-home lab tests work?** Collect your sample with the included kit, mail it back in the pre-paid envelope, and access results securely in the Patientory app.

**Is my personal health information secure?** Yes — state-of-the-art encryption keeps your data private.
