# Pre-Diabetes Prevention Program — Patientory

> Prevent diabetes before it starts with innovative tools, personalized insights, and real-time guidance: smart tracking of your health metrics, expert support from health professionals, and a data-driven approach to preventing Type 2 diabetes. Part of Patientory's Chronic Care Management services.

Page: https://patientory.com/services/pre-diabetes

## How it works

1. **Enroll and get started** — sign up and receive tools tailored to your health goals.
2. **Receive your health kit** — a Google Fitbit, Smart Scale, Diabetes Test (HbA1c), and Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) delivered to your doorstep. Devices come preconfigured and ready to use.
3. **Personalized health insights** — sync your devices to the Patientory app for real-time data, actionable insights, and personalized feedback.
4. **Daily coaching & support** — expert advice, lifestyle tips, and motivation from health coaches.
5. **Achieve lasting results** — build healthy habits, monitor your metrics, and reduce your risk of developing diabetes.

## What's in the health kit

- **Google Fitbit** — activity, sleep patterns, and heart-rate tracking
- **Smart Scale** — weight and body-composition monitoring
- **Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)** — real-time blood glucose feedback throughout the day
- **Diabetes Test** — accurate HbA1c measurement to tailor your personal care plan

## Program benefits

- **Comprehensive health tracking** — activity, heart rate, glucose levels, and weight trends in one place
- **Personalized recommendations** — tailored diet and exercise plans based on your health data
- **Continuous glucose monitoring** — understand your body's response to foods, stress, and activities
- **Guided support & coaching** — certified health coaches with regular check-ins
- **Measurable results** — reduce Type 2 diabetes risk with preventive, technology-backed measures
- **Data security** — HIPAA-compliant, encrypted health data

## FAQ

**Who is this program for?** Individuals at risk of pre-diabetes, or anyone who wants to proactively manage their health to avoid diabetes.

**What if I already own a Fitbit or Smart Scale?** The program integrates with existing devices.

**How soon will I see results?** Most participants see improvements in blood glucose, weight, and overall health within the first 4–6 weeks.
