# About Patientory — Changing healthcare from the ground up

> Patientory Inc. is a population health data management and analytics company empowering people to take charge of their own health. We're revolutionizing the way healthcare stakeholders and patients interact and gain access to information, cutting out the layers and processes that are stumbling blocks in care coordination.

Page: https://patientory.com/about

## We believe better healthcare starts with the people

Patientory empowers you with control of your health data, so you can maximize your health and earn personalized reward-based $PTOY token payments.

## Culture at Patientory

- **Mission** — To empower people with actionable data-driven insights for improved health outcomes and well-being.
- **Vision** — Build a healthier future for all by becoming the home of patient data.
- **Key belief** — Impacting population health starts with impacting one person at a time.
- **Values** — Empowerment, Clarity, Security, Integrity.

## Products

- **Patientory mobile app** (iOS & Android) — a digital health passport wallet: medical records storage on a HIPAA-compliant blockchain-enabled platform, an AI health coach, Health Score tracking, and $PTOY rewards. Download: https://patientory.com/download
- **NEITH enterprise platform** — population health management data & analytics for enterprises, enabling improved healthcare outcomes through access, preventative and prescriptive data management.
