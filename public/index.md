# Patientory — Own your health. Get rewarded for it.

> Patientory is your digital health passport: medical records, an AI health coach, and a data marketplace that pays you in crypto for every healthy choice you make. The app is live on iOS and Android. Patientory is HIPAA & GDPR compliant and secured by the PTOYMatrix blockchain.

## The Wallet — one wallet, every part of your health

Securely connected. AI-powered. Personalized. Rewarded.

- **AI Health Coach** — Your AI health coach reads your records, wearables and habits, then builds a personalized care plan that adapts every day.
- **Rewards** — Healthy choices earn $PTOY. Hit your goals, share your data on your terms, and watch the rewards stack up.
- **Medical Records** — Every record, one wallet. Your complete medical history — encrypted, portable, and always in your pocket.
- **Health Score** — One number that tells the whole story. Track your Health Score in real time and see exactly which habits move it up.
- **Habit Tracking** — Hydration, sleep & daily streaks. Simple trackers keep the small wins visible — and rewarded.

## How Patientory works — three steps to owning your data

1. **Connect** — Link your providers, records and wearables. Everything lands in one encrypted wallet secured by the PTOYMatrix blockchain.
2. **Understand** — Your AI health coach turns raw data into a Health Score and a care plan you can actually follow.
3. **Earn** — Healthy actions and consented data sharing earn $PTOY — spend it on cost-reduced care across the marketplace.
