# CarePharmacy — Prescription Savings Card — Patientory

> Get the free Patientory Prescription Insurance Savings Card and enjoy significant discounts — up to 80% — on eligible medications at over 60,000 participating pharmacies across the United States. No fees, no registration, no eligibility restrictions.

Page: https://patientory.com/carepharmacy

## How CarePharmacy works

1. **Get your card** — download or request your free savings card.
2. **Find a pharmacy** — locate a participating pharmacy near you.
3. **Present your card** — show it to the pharmacist when filling prescriptions.
4. **Save instantly** — immediate savings on eligible medications.

## Benefits

- **Save up to 80%** — significant discounts on both brand-name and generic medications
- **No fees or registration** — completely free to use, no personal information required
- **For everyone** — available to all patients regardless of insurance status; no income, age, or eligibility restrictions
- **Nationwide coverage** — accepted at over 60,000 participating pharmacies across the United States
- **Broad medication coverage** — discounts on a wide range of prescriptions, including many not covered by insurance
- **Digital access** — save the card on your smartphone
