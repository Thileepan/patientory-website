## What the app does

- Store your complete medical records — encrypted, portable, secured by the PTOYMatrix blockchain
- AI health coach with a personalized, adaptive care plan
- Real-time Health Score and habit tracking (hydration, sleep, streaks)
- Earn $PTOY rewards for healthy choices and consented data sharing
- Access the Care Health Data Marketplace: CarePlan, CareTrials, CareGLP, CareMeds, CarePharmacy, CareLabs
