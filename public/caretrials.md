# CareTrials — Clinical Trial Opportunities — Patientory

> CareTrials matches Patientory Wallet users to clinical trials that fit their health profile. Clinical trials are needed globally to reduce disease burdens by helping to develop safe and effective new therapies and vaccines. Patients are eligible to be included in a study only if they meet all of the required criteria — and sharing your trial registration confirmation earns in-app rewards ($PTOY).

Page: https://patientory.com/caretrials
Trial sponsors (pharma/CRO): book a discovery call at https://calendly.com/patientory-services/discovery-call — see also https://patientory.com/enterprises/clinical-research-organization.md

## Active clinical trials

- **Treatment for uncontrolled Asthma (TANGO study, sponsored by Chiesi)** — evaluates the outcomes and safety of a potential new treatment to help patients with uncontrolled asthma. Open to people diagnosed with asthma, aged 18–75.

The list of active trials changes over time — see the live page for current opportunities.

## Completed trials

Patientory has supported recruitment for studies including: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, Head and Neck Cancer, Mycobacterium Infections, Hepatic Encephalopathy, Cytomegalovirus (CMV), Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer, Hereditary Angioedema, NSCLC/Melanoma, and Relapsed or Refractory Peripheral T-Cell Lymphoma.

## How it works for patients

1. Download the Patientory app (https://patientory.com/download) and connect your health records.
2. Your wallet determines the best-fit clinical trial opportunities for your profile.
3. Register for a matching trial and share your registration confirmation in the app to receive rewards.
